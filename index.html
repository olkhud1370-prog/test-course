    02The framework

    The art of recommending

    Select each step to explore it.

    01

    Get to know the reader.

    • Ask what they enjoy reading.
    • Find out what kind of reading experience they want.
    • Avoid assuming their preferences.
    02

    Identify what matters to the reader.

    • Pay attention to genre, mood, and pacing.
    • Notice preferences and deal-breakers.
    • Clarify anything that is unclear.
    03

    Connect the book to the reader.

    • Recommend a title that reflects their preferences.
    • Explain why you think it is a good fit.
    • Give the customer room to decide.
